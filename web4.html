Web 4.0

Web 4.0 is the fourth generation of the web, also known as the symbiotic web or intelligent web. It aims to create a deeper interaction between humans and machines using artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, and augmented reality. Web 4.0 envisions machines being able to understand and respond to human needs proactively.
